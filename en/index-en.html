Process Optimization & Automated Workflows

Context: Unimed CNU | Private Health Operations

The Bottleneck: Manual protocols scattered across multiple legacy systems resulted in an Average Handling Time (AHT) of 5 minutes per request, overloading the team.

The Solution: Design and validation of an end-to-end automated workflow integrating migration to Salesforce, data capture, and multichannel notifications (WhatsApp and email), eliminating repetitive human intervention.

The Result: Reduction of AHT from 5 to 1.5–2 minutes per protocol, immediately freeing operational capacity.

  • Python
  • Salesforce
  • Workflow Automation
  • Systems Integration
  • Process Optimization
-17% AHT (Average Handling Time)

Generative AI Implementation in Customer Support Operations

Context: Unimed CNU | Scaled to 150+ employees

The Bottleneck: High volume of queries about complex regulatory rules (TUSS tables and medication guidelines), combined with the need to maintain empathy and accuracy in customer support.

The Solution: Creation and orchestration of 2 LLM-based virtual assistants (Gemini) — a semantic search engine for care guidelines and a tone analyzer for real-time support and triage.

The Result: 17% reduction in AHT and an increase in the satisfaction score from 8.2 to 8.5, demonstrating the impact of AI applied at the operational front line.

  • Generative AI
  • LLMs (Gemini)
  • Prompt Engineering
  • Customer Support Optimization
5x Faster Routine (10 min → 2 min)

Operational Data Extraction & Cleaning Automation

Context: Unimed CNU | Intelligence & Planning

The Bottleneck: Dependence on slow, manual data extraction routines to monitor volume, traffic, and compliance with critical SLAs.

The Solution: Design of new service KPIs and development of Python (Pandas) routines combined with structured SQL Server queries for automated extraction and cleaning of large datasets.

The Result: Reduced routine processing time from 10 to just 2 minutes, ensuring consistent and integrated data for faster decision-making.

  • Python (Pandas)
  • SQL Server
  • Data Modeling
  • KPIs & SLAs
  • Data Cleaning
